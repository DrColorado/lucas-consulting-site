<div class="about-text">
<p>Lucas Consultant Services LLC is an independently owned consulting firm with decades of experience in the core areas of technology, academics, and business administration. In the technology area, our focus is on systems design and development, with a wide range of software and hardware experience for large organizations as well as small businesses, start-ups, and individual users. In academics, we've served in senior leadership positions in large and small institutions of higher learning, conducting research, managing projects, improving institutional effectiveness, academic quality, assessment, learning outcomes, and student success. In business administration, we've worked with many international businesses, both within the US and in Europe, Asia, and South America, improving organizational performance and stability, profitability, employee satisfaction, risk management, brand expansion, and strategic planning for large companies as well as small businesses.</p>

<p>Although we spent many years in the corporate world, the last decade has gradually brought us closer to the individual user, and today, we most frequently serve the day-to-day needs of those in the communities around us. We use data analytics, business intelligence, and the latest in artificial intelligence technologies to keep your personal goals and objectives both easier to manage and more comfortably within your reach. We invite you to learn more about us. We're confident we can offer a product or service to make your life easier.</p>
